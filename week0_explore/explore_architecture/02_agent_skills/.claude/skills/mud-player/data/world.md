# World Map

## Known Rooms

- **Temple Of Midgaard** (starting room) -- exits: n e s w d. ATM machine here.
- **Temple Square** (d from Temple Of Midgaard) -- exits: n e s w. Clerics'
  Guild to the west, Grunting Boar Inn to the east, fountain here. South goes
  to Market Square.
- **Market Square** (s from Temple Square) -- exits: n e s w. The famous
  Square of Midgaard, statue in the middle. A Peacekeeper and a janitor NPC
  are usually here. West leads to Main Street, south to the common square.
- **Main Street** (w from Market Square) -- exits: n e s w. Armory entrance
  to the south, the Bakery to the north.
- **The Bakery** (n from Main Street) -- exits: s only. A sign on the counter
  gives instructions (`read sign`): `buy` purchases bread/pastry, `list`
  shows the menu. The baker NPC is here. Menu (via `list`):
  1) danish pastry -- 7 coins (unlimited)
  2) bread -- 14 coins (unlimited)
  3) waybread -- 73 coins (unlimited)
- **Main Street (eastern end)** -- exits: n e s w. Weapon shop to the north,
  Guild of Swordsmen to the south. The eastern exit leads out of town.
- **The Entrance Hall To The Guild Of Swordsmen** -- exits: n e. ATM machine
  here. A knight is guarding the entrance. North goes to Main Street, east to
  the bar.
- **The Bar Of Swordsmen** -- exits: s w. Bulletin board mounted on wall. A
  waiter is here. South goes to the practice yard, west to the entrance hall.
- **The Tournament And Practice Yard** (Swordsmen's Guild training area) --
  exits: n d. The guildmaster NPC is here sharpening an axe. North goes to the
  bar. Well leads down (unexplored).
- **Great Field Of Midgaard** (north of the temple areas, accessed by going
  north through the temple) -- peaceful countryside with grass and oak trees.
- **The Entrance To The Newbie Zone** -- exits: n w. West leads back to Great
  Field. North enters the Newbie Zone.
- **The Beginning Of The Passage** (Newbie Zone) -- exits: e s. The newbie
  monster NPC is/was here (practice dummy for combat training).

## Skills/Abilities Confirmed

- **Kick skill**: Confirmed at "poor" level with the guildmaster. Successfully
  used in combat against the newbie monster (delivered killing blow: "You
  pierce the newbie monster's heart"). Gained 149 XP for defeating the monster.

## Additional Newbie Zone Areas (Recent Exploration)

- **A Small Room** (south from Dirty Hallway, south exit initially closed/locked) -- contains Newbie Guard NPC and a well with a secure locked grate. Exits: n, e (blocked), d (grate locked). Possible entrance to deeper dungeon.
- **Dirty Hallway doorway to south**: Door can be opened via "open door" command when in hallway.
- Red Room: Rumored to exist in Newbie Zone, location not yet found. Potentially connected to locked well or deeper areas.
